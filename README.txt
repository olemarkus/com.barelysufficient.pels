Charge your car on cheap electricity, turn solar surplus into hot water and keep your biggest loads working together. PELS coordinates EV chargers such as Easee and Zaptec, heat pumps, thermostats, water heaters and home batteries across brands.

Take control of your available power. Capacity limit manages hourly or 15-minute tariff peaks; Grid import limit manages the power you draw from the grid. Use either or both. PELS lowers charging current, adjusts heating and pauses lower-priority devices, then resumes them when power becomes available. It follows whole-home consumption through Homey Energy, a meter reader or a Flow. Additional metered homes can have their own limits.

Make cheap hours work for you. Use prices from Homey Energy, Power by the Hour or Flows, and bring time-of-use rates and network charges into the plan through Homey Energy or Flows. With Smart tasks, choose a goal and deadline: the car at 80% by 07:00, a warm living room at homecoming or energy for your water heater. PELS plans the cheapest hours. Select the cars using your charger and PELS reads their battery levels directly. Heating and cooling also follow 15-minute prices.

Use more of your own solar. Surplus heats water, warms or cools your home and charges your car, with charging current following the sun. PELS learns from production to use spare solar in zero-export setups too. Your export price, including negative prices, shapes the plan. Usage shows production, self-consumption, export and avoided grid costs.

Your home battery joins the same plan: storing leftover solar, reducing charging when devices need the power and discharging to hold your limit. Your priorities decide how the battery and appliances work together.

Home, Away and Night modes fit your routines. Daily budget spreads consumption across the day, while weather learning shows how tomorrow looks. Dashboard widgets show available power, budget and Smart tasks, and let you start tasks directly. Flows connect it all to your automations.

Open Homey > More > Apps > PELS > Settings. Follow the guided setup and preview your plan in Simulation mode before switching on automatic control.

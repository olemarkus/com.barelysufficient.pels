# Price Inputs

`lib/price/` fetches and combines spot, grid, Homey Energy, Flow, and Power by the Hour prices. It produces price facts; it does not choose devices or actuate them.

## Periods and hours

Published prices may be hourly or quarter-hourly. `getCombinedPricePeriods()` serves the period in force for the current price level; `getCombinedHourlyPrices()` serves duration-weighted hours for budgets, smart tasks, tariff and Flow logic. Keep the types distinct so an hourly consumer cannot count 96 quarters as 96 hours. A price refresh alone does not trigger a plan rebuild; the next meter reading picks it up. An owner settings change can trigger one.

## Storage and source boundaries

- Persisted `pricesBySlot` remains one entry per local hour for older app versions. Sub-hourly values use `pricesByPeriod`. Price caches live in the userdata database through typed stores, not new Homey settings blobs.
- Power by the Hour publishes future slots. Rotate cached days before merging; an unreadable cache read is not an empty day. Drop its forecast-marked slots at the source adapter, before they can be treated as market prices.
- Consumers receive resolved prices and levels without source-specific branches. `price_scheme` has one shared type and read policy in `packages/contracts/` and `packages/shared-domain/src/settings/` because runtime and UI both use it.
- Keep price as a leaf module under `.dependency-cruiser.cjs`; do not import device, planner, objectives, observer, or executor behavior here.

`nettleieFallbackData.generated.ts` is generated by `npm run build:nettleie-fallback` and is not hand-edited.

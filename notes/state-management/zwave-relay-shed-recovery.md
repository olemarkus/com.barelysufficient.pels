# Recovering a Z-Wave relay shed

When PELS needs to switch off a metered load, a missing Z-Wave acknowledgement and an OFF relay that still reports substantial draw both call for another look at the electrical result. They provide different evidence about the command. Use the next whole-home meter decision to protect capacity. Allow at most one same-value OFF while Homey shows the relay OFF during a continuous shed episode. A load that still reports ON remains available for ordinary, paced shedding.

## Scope and rationale

Apply recovery to PELS-issued native, absolute on/off commands that shed a managed metered relay. Resolve a positive Z-Wave identification from the Homey device’s validated `flags` array containing `zwave`. The exact Homey status token `TRANSMIT_COMPLETE_NO_ACK` from that native write also identifies the failed transmission. A successful write needs positive device identification to qualify. A missing flag leaves the device usable through its ordinary path. Device class, brand, generic HTTP 500s and translated error prose are insufficient evidence. Flow actions, starts, charger controls, stepped loads, temperature setbacks and lifecycle hand-backs keep their existing semantics.

Homey publishes a capability value after the owning driver handles the request. Its echo can settle the logical OFF while the electrical result is still unknown. Homey Core 4.6.5 forwards a same-value write to the owning driver. A repeat can therefore request OFF again without turning the circuit on first. Whether that request produces additional electrical relief on a particular model requires measurement. PELS’s normal device refresh reads the Homey device model. Physical readback is available to controllers and owning drivers with radio access. For example, openHAB supports polling after a command. PELS can use the observations it already receives without adding a radio-management permission. [Homey device fields](https://athombv.github.io/node-homey-api/HomeyAPIV3Local.ManagerDevices.Device.html) · [Homey Z-Wave errors](https://support.homey.app/hc/en-us/articles/10225734620188-Common-Z-Wave-error-messages) · [openHAB Z-Wave binding](https://www.openhab.org/addons/bindings/zwave/#polling)

## Decision on a new meter reading

| Evidence after the original OFF | Proposed behavior |
|---|---|
| Native write returns the exact NO_ACK status | Record that delivery is uncertain. Keep ordinary shedding available while the relay reports ON and draws power. After a NO_ACK, pace further requests to that relay at most once per confirmation interval, measured from the last dispatch. While the failure is unresolved, its projected relief is unconfirmed. Other eligible loads can cover the deficit in priority order. If this is the only lever, later meter decisions can retry it while the deficit persists. |
| Homey shows OFF and the only device watts predate the command | Preserve the accepted reading and its timestamp. Show that it was last measured before switch-off. Whole-home metering can establish that the household has enough relief. |
| Homey shows OFF and post-settle measurements establish sustained draw | Flag the discrepancy. Consider one further OFF for remaining pressure under the evidence rule below. Preserve the observed binary state. |
| Device draw falls to its normal idle level, or pressure clears | Suppress the recovery write. If only part of the load disappeared, the next measured plan deals with the remaining deficit. |
| The same-value OFF has been issued and the relay continues to report OFF with substantial draw | Mark the same-value allowance spent for this episode. Continue selecting other eligible loads and allow the existing shortfall path to report exhausted options. Show the unresolved condition on the device. |

The relay settle interval is 90 seconds from the ordinary OFF dispatch, using `CONTROL_COMMAND_CONFIRMATION_MS`. An ordinary retry while the relay reports ON starts a new settling interval and retains the original pre-command baseline. Eligibility for a same-value OFF starts at the end of that interval. For successive NO_ACKs while ON, the 90-second pacing clock starts at the most recent dispatch. On exact NO_ACK, withdraw that attempt’s prospective credit immediately. The next eligible whole-home reading can select a healthy alternative even inside the original 30-second pending-relief window. Use that new reading to account for any load that has since disappeared. Whole-home pressure and candidate availability are recomputed on each eligible meter reading, including when there is no other candidate. Waiting for a new reading follows the configured meter cadence.

## Electrical evidence

Capture the last accepted direct device watts and their measurement time before dispatch. Retain the original baseline throughout the episode. For an acknowledged OFF, compare against direct watts both measured and received after the settle interval. A cached refresh with the same measurement timestamp, configured watts, estimates and an undated sample cannot advance the comparison.

Proposed initial thresholds for high-draw relays are a pre-command baseline of at least **250 W**, residual draw of at least **250 W**, and a fall of **less than 30%** of the baseline. The watt floor excludes small standby loads from automatic recovery. These are policy starting values to evaluate in diagnostics. Exactly 30% is partial relief outside this retry trigger. A fall of 40% from 2 kW still leaves 1.2 kW for the house meter to account for.

One qualifying reading flags the discrepancy. A reading captured after dispatch but before settling can be shown as post-command telemetry. Automatic actuation requires evidence beyond the interval. Quiet devices may never produce that evidence. Other loads remain available. For an automatic repeat against reported OFF, require either two qualifying readings with distinct source timestamps after settling, or one qualifying reading corroborated by advancing device energy. Use two actual, dated, monotonic kWh readings wholly within the post-settle OFF interval, from the same endpoint and counter epoch. In the first delivery, collect these as supporting diagnostics. Enable this alternative actuation gate only where the transport supplies counter resolution and sample provenance. Require the lower bound of average power over that interval, accounting for counter resolution, to meet the same residual and percentage thresholds. Discard a reset, delayed pre-command reading or indeterminate resolution. Watts and kWh can come from the same meter. An isolated whole-home fall can corroborate electrical relief for diagnostics. Overlapping load changes cannot identify which circuit changed. No retry needs to wait for an energy counter when two fresh watt readings suffice.

## Ownership and lifetime

- **Device transport** resolves Z-Wave identity and the exact write error at the native Homey boundary. Preserve the original error for support. Expose a typed outcome through the actuator so the executor can distinguish NO_ACK, an HTTP timeout, a declined request and an acknowledged write.
- **Observer** retains binary state, direct power, energy and their source times independently. Use the existing dated power observations. Preserve a genuine zero. Re-reading an accepted value cannot manufacture another sample. Power evidence never changes logical on/off.
- **Executor** owns the original OFF, its baseline, dispatch time, outcome and single additional attempt. Open the record before awaiting the write so an early echo can correlate. Route every write through the existing actuator and control-contest accounting. Record logical-ON retries separately from the one same-value allowance. The executor record survives an OFF echo clearing the observer’s pending entry. An echoed OFF or a later NO_ACK on the repeat cannot reset its allowance. Consume the same-value allowance before issuing the repeat and recheck authority, observation revision, pending writes and the latest opposing intent. One executor-owned predicate must cover outstanding-work detection, the early already-OFF check in shed dispatch, and binary preflight. Require the current measured decision to authorize a controlled binary shed, the original binding and current authority. An opposing pending intent blocks recovery. The ordinary actuator invocation and contest reporting then apply. Issue OFF directly.
- **Planner** owns selection and pressure. Preserve priority order. Today a failed write clears the pending binary entry, so `buildBinaryCandidate` can lose the `unconfirmedRelief` signal on the next walk. Project the unresolved NO_ACK and its next eligible time as a distinct delivery outcome. In scalar and phase selection, give a retry zero prospective relief. Reserve `unconfirmedRelief` for an actual pending request. Invalidate the failed attempt’s contribution to pending relief and both shortfall measures as soon as its NO_ACK arrives. Match the dispatch to its own latched decision. Preserve unrelated pending credits and any historical attribution needed for overlapping meter falls. In `shortfallVerdict.ts`, the failed outcome contributes zero to `hasElectricalReliefInFlight`, including when a retry is selected, and a paced-out relay contributes zero to `resolveReliefLeftKw`. An eligible retry can still be issued. Shortfall reporting resumes as soon as existing guards allow when every useful lever is exhausted. A later accepted ordinary write hands confirmation back to the normal pending path.
- **Already-OFF recovery** is convergence on a currently authorized shed. Carry the planner’s permission with the existing controlled binary OFF intent only when a new whole-home reading still calls for electrical relief on the relevant constraint. A hold with no residual pressure, a restore-admission hold with no binary intent, a non-meter rebuild and an old committed plan grant no permission. If the relay leaves the final OFF intent, evidence alone cannot put it back. The executor can surface outstanding work for an unchanged authorized intent after evidence arrives. This repeat offers zero new prospective relief and does not re-enter normal shed ranking. Device events update evidence. The next meter decision owns actuation.
- **Episode lifetime** follows the continuous need to hold that device OFF. An explicit release, a superseding ON decision, loss of management, a changed native binding or device removal closes it. A genuine non-PELS ON observation invalidates the previous OFF evidence and permits normal binary convergence on a new decision. The repeat allowance stays spent until the continuous shed ends. An omitted candidate, another NO_ACK, an echoed OFF or another meter build cannot rearm it. Keep recovery state bounded to managed devices. On restart discard old evidence and queued intents. A relay already observed OFF at startup needs a new ordinary shed episode before a same-value repeat can qualify. A new ordinary OFF after restart starts a new process-local allowance.

## Delivery and acceptance

1. Ship typed NO_ACK handling, candidate fallback and the distinction between pre-OFF and post-OFF power in device status. Record one structured outcome per incident or material transition with command result, sample ages, pre/post watts, actual household relief and whether other sheds overlapped.
2. Evaluate the 30% detector and bounded repeat using diagnostics. Track eligible sheds, fresh evidence, spontaneous recovery, and reductions delivered by alternative loads. Compare the post-retry results using fresh device draw and household power. A physical measurement can establish whether a relay retransmits and reduces the load.
3. Cover the normal meter → plan → executor → actuator path with synthetic cases. A rejected first-choice relay yields to a healthy alternative. When the only relay is paced out after NO_ACK, a new meter reading with persistent deficit can open the existing shortfall incident even within the original 30-second window. If the load has already fallen, that same reading suppresses escalation. The relay can still retry at the bounded rate on subsequent meter decisions. The 90-second wait cannot delay an eligible backup. An accepted OFF with pre-command watts gets none. Two independently dated high readings can qualify a repeat. Duplicate snapshots, meter resets, late zero, pressure clearing, stale meter input and revoked authority suppress it. A second failure, a new plan during dispatch and an echo during dispatch cannot duplicate the write. Restart drops the previous intent. A Flow switch, non-Z-Wave relay, generic 500 and stepped charger retain ordinary control behavior.

Focused coverage belongs in the existing binary dispatch and actuator integration specs and the pending-relief, shortfall and device-status unit specs. Validate shortfall through the capacity guard as well as the selection result. The end-to-end acceptance criterion is that PELS can relieve a real remaining deficit while a problematic relay supplies zero assumed additional relief and receives at most one already-OFF repeat per episode. Ordinary shed attempts while the device reports ON remain paced and measurable.
